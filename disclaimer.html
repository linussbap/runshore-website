Website disclaimer

This notice explains the status and intended use of RunShore website information, AI-generated material, technical examples, demonstrations and evidence.

Draft for UK legal reviewLast reviewed: 12 November 2026Provisional basis: England and Wales
Draft — not approved for public launch. The legal operator's full name, business address, contact email and correct governing-law jurisdiction have not been supplied in this draft. Complete those details and obtain review by a solicitor qualified in the relevant UK jurisdiction before publishing this page.

RunShore is currently presented as a product/project name. This notice applies to the RunShore website and the information made available through its product pages, documentation, demonstrations, slide shows, Client App walkthroughs, evidence library, ASK AI and LEARN material (together, the Website Materials). A separate written agreement may govern access to a paid or contracted RunShore service. If so, that agreement applies to the relevant service and takes precedence where its terms expressly say so.

1. Informational purpose and product status

The Website Materials are provided to explain the RunShore project, its intended capabilities, technical approach, learning materials and development progress. Some pages may describe capabilities that are planned, being developed, under evaluation or not yet available to every user. A description, roadmap item, screen illustration or proposed workflow is not a promise that the feature is available, production-ready, accredited or suitable for a particular purpose.

RunShore will identify material that is illustrative, recorded, simulated, planned or in development where that status is relevant. Visitors should check the date, environment, scope and accompanying explanation of a demonstration or evidence item before drawing conclusions from it.

2. Technical information and professional judgement

Technical explanations, architecture examples, configuration samples, scripts, command-line instructions and operational guidance are general information. They are not a substitute for a competent assessment of your own environment, security requirements, change controls, contractual obligations or regulatory duties.

Technology versions, defaults, product features, cloud services, prices, documentation and security recommendations change. Although reasonable care should be taken when preparing the Website Materials, information may become incomplete, out of date or unsuitable for a particular environment. Check the relevant product owner's or maintainer's current documentation and test proposed changes in an appropriately isolated, non-production environment before use.

Before performing a change to a database, operating system, Kubernetes cluster, cloud account or other system, understand its effect, obtain required authorisation, review dependencies and recovery arrangements, and verify that suitable backups can be restored. Commands and examples that can delete, overwrite, expose or modify data or infrastructure require particular care.

3. ASK AI and generated responses

ASK AI may use third-party artificial-intelligence services, including Anthropic and OpenAI, depending on the configuration made available by RunShore. AI-generated answers can be incorrect, incomplete, misleading, outdated or inconsistent with the current behaviour of a product or service. A generated answer, code sample, diagnosis, command, citation or suggested remediation must not be treated as verified merely because an AI system produced it.

Where source references are provided, check that the cited page exists, is current and supports the relevant statement. Official product-owner or maintainer documentation should be treated as the primary technical reference, but it must still be checked against the version and configuration in use.

Do not rely on ASK AI as the sole basis for production changes, security decisions, database recovery, migration, financial commitments, legal or regulatory decisions, or other high-impact actions. Obtain appropriate human review and use your organisation's normal approval and change-management processes.

Do not submit passwords, API keys, private keys, access tokens, customer records, confidential business information, personal data or other restricted material to ASK AI unless the relevant RunShore service has been expressly configured and approved for that information and its handling is explained in the applicable Privacy Notice and service terms. Never paste live secrets into a public demonstration or learning exercise.

4. LEARN and educational material

LEARN is intended to support technical understanding through explanations, examples, learning paths, exercises and knowledge checks. Unless RunShore expressly states otherwise in writing, the material is not an accredited course, regulated qualification, professional certification, formal assessment or guarantee of employment, competence or a particular learning outcome.

Learners should check official documentation and apply appropriate supervision and safety controls when practising with operating systems, databases, cloud platforms and production technologies. Educational content does not replace institution-specific requirements, professional advice or workplace procedures.

5. Commands, scripts, infrastructure and cloud costs

Examples involving shell commands, Bash or zsh scripts, Go, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, databases, backup and recovery, monitoring or cloud infrastructure are provided for learning and illustration. Their effect depends on the environment, permissions, software versions, configuration and data involved.

Cloud and third-party services may incur charges, consume quotas, create resources, transfer data or change service availability. A sample architecture or demonstration does not establish the price or suitability of a real deployment. Check current provider pricing, service limits, regional availability, security requirements and operating costs before provisioning resources. Review infrastructure plans and commands before applying them, and remove test resources only after confirming that doing so is safe and authorised.

You remain responsible for ensuring that you have permission to access and test the systems you use. Do not run examples against systems, networks, data or accounts without appropriate authority. Nothing in this notice removes any duty or liability that RunShore may have under applicable law or an applicable contract.

6. Demonstrations, screenshots and evidence

Demonstrations, slide shows, screenshots, terminal captures and other evidence items may represent a particular point in time, software version, test environment, configuration or selected workflow. They may be recorded rather than live. A screenshot is not, by itself, proof of continuous availability, production scale, security certification, performance under all conditions or successful operation in another environment.

Where practical, evidence should identify its scenario, environment, date, scope and validation method. Sensitive information should be removed before publication. Do not infer that a feature is complete merely because a design image, roadmap, slide or proposed workflow depicts it.

7. Cloud providers, products and third-party services

References to Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Kubernetes, Docker, database products, monitoring products, AI providers and other third-party services are made for identification, explanation, comparison or demonstration. Their names, logos and marks belong to their respective owners.

Unless a specific partnership or authorisation is expressly stated, RunShore does not claim to be sponsored, approved, certified, endorsed by or affiliated with those third parties. Product names and comparisons do not amount to a recommendation that one provider is suitable for every workload. Features, prices, service levels and terms are determined by the relevant provider and may change.

The Website may link to third-party websites and documentation. RunShore does not control those sites or their content, availability, security, privacy practices or terms. A link is provided for convenience and does not imply endorsement. Check the third party's current terms and notices before using its services.

Unless a page states otherwise, the RunShore name, original website text, original diagrams, design elements and RunShore-specific materials are presented as part of the RunShore project. Third-party product names, logos, documentation and other materials remain the property of their respective owners. Their inclusion does not transfer ownership or grant a licence beyond any rights provided by the relevant owner or applicable law.

Do not reproduce, redistribute or commercially exploit material from the Website in a way that infringes intellectual-property rights. Where RunShore republishes or refers to third-party material, the relevant source and licence or permission should be checked.

9. Availability and changes

The Website and its materials may be updated, corrected, suspended, withdrawn or reorganised as the RunShore project develops. The static website, demonstrations and links may not always be available or current. This statement does not promise uninterrupted access or remove any service obligations that apply under a separate contract or mandatory law.

10. Liability and statutory rights

This disclaimer is intended to explain the scope and limitations of general website information. It is not intended to exclude or restrict liability where doing so would be unlawful, or to remove or reduce any statutory rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.

Nothing in this notice excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any other liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. Nothing in this notice affects consumer rights or remedies that apply under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 or other applicable law.

Any limitation of responsibility in a separate contract must be considered on its wording, context and applicable law. A broad disclaimer does not automatically make an exclusion of liability enforceable. Consumer terms and notices must satisfy the applicable fairness and transparency requirements.

11. Privacy, personal data and cookies

This disclaimer is not a Privacy Notice or Cookie Notice. Before collecting personal data through contact forms, accounts, analytics, ASK AI, LEARN or other features, RunShore must publish a separate Privacy Notice explaining, as applicable, who controls the data, what is collected, why it is used, the lawful basis, recipients and AI service providers, retention, international transfers, individual rights and how to raise a concern with the Information Commission (which continues to operate under the ICO name).

Any use of cookies or similar technologies must be assessed separately under applicable privacy and electronic communications rules. Where consent is required for non-essential technologies, an appropriate consent mechanism and clear information must be provided before those technologies are used.

Do not assume that the presence of this disclaimer means all privacy, data protection or cookie requirements have been completed.

Before public launch, this page and the website footer must identify the legal person or entity responsible for the Website and provide the contact information required by applicable law. This normally includes an operator name, geographic business address and a working email address, together with registration details where applicable.

13. Governing law and jurisdiction

This draft has been prepared on the provisional basis that England and Wales is the appropriate governing-law jurisdiction. That assumption must be confirmed against the identity and place of establishment of the legal operator and the nature of the relevant relationship before publication. Scotland and Northern Ireland have distinct legal systems, and mandatory consumer protections or jurisdictional rules may apply regardless of a chosen governing-law clause.

Nothing in this section removes a right to rely on mandatory legal protections or a jurisdictional rule that cannot lawfully be excluded.

This notice may be revised as RunShore's product, services, legal operator, data practices and website content change. The date at the top of the page should be updated when a material revision is published. Visitors should review the current version when returning to the Website.
