A platform proposition for database and cloud operations

RunShore is being developed to bring database assessment, platform operations, observability and AI-assisted technical learning into a connected engineering experience.

Explore product material → →

The problem space

Database and platform teams work across fragmented tools, cloud services, scripts, monitoring systems and operational documentation. Understanding the whole lifecycle—from provisioning and assessment to observability and recovery—can be difficult.

The proposed response

RunShore aims to provide a connected interface for database platform deployment, assessment, observability registration and evidence-led workflows, with AI-assisted technical guidance.

Potential customer groups

  • Small and medium organisations operating database workloads.
  • Managed service and platform engineering teams.
  • Organisations modernising database estates.
  • Training providers and education institutions.

Development priorities

  • Reliability and repeatability of supported workflows.
  • Clear capability boundaries across cloud platforms.
  • Security, governance and evidence quality.
  • Structured AI assistance and practical learning.
  • Customer discovery and validation of commercial demand.

Potential value areas

Operational consistency, reduced context switching, repeatable assessments, better visibility of database workloads and accessible technical knowledge. These are product hypotheses to validate with users, not claimed measured outcomes.

What to evaluate

Review the platform architecture, demonstration scenarios, delivery roadmap and evidence. Funding discussions should be grounded in validated customer needs, delivery milestones and a realistic cost model.
