01 · The platform

One place to connect database engineering, cloud operations and observability.

RunShore is being developed around repeatable workflows, evidence-led assessment and practical technical learning.

Database platformsCloud infrastructureKubernetesObservability
02 · Database engineering

Support multiple database technologies and deployment patterns.

  • PostgreSQL and CloudNativePG.
  • MySQL and Percona XtraDB Cluster.
  • MongoDB and Percona Server for MongoDB.
  • SAP ASE / Sybase as a migration-source and assessment technology.
03 · Cloud and orchestration

Compare and operate across the major cloud ecosystems.

  • AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud.
  • EKS, AKS and GKE as managed Kubernetes options.
  • Terraform for infrastructure provisioning.
  • Docker and Kubernetes for container platforms.
04 · Observability and validation

Connect operational activity with telemetry and evidence.

  • Percona Monitoring and Management.
  • New Relic and Splunk integrations.
  • Workload activity and metric interpretation.
  • Validation records and evidence-backed demonstrations.
05 · ASK AI and LEARN

Turn platform knowledge into technical understanding.

  • Anthropic and OpenAI as supported AI provider options.
  • Original explanations with official owner or maintainer sources.
  • macOS and Linux command-line learning.
  • Guided lessons, exercises and cloud comparison.
06 · Evidence and audiences

One product story, three ways to explore it.

  • Recruiters: inspect engineering implementation.
  • Funding organisations: assess the proposition and roadmap.
  • Education establishments: explore practical learning and partnership opportunities.
  • Documentation explains, demonstrations show, evidence substantiates.
