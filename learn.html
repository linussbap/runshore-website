LEARN

Practical technical learning, with ASK AI as the tutor

LEARN is a tab in the RunShore Client App. Each lesson explains a topic in the learner's chosen environment, links to the official documentation, and ends with a quiz and practical exercises that ASK AI checks and explains.

How ASK AI works →
Built and in the Client App

Area 1: Operating systems

10 lessons across macOS, Linux, Bash, zsh and macOS-versus-Linux differences.

macOS or Linux, one switch

Every lesson has a macOS / Linux switch. Commands and explanations change to match the chosen system, including the BSD and GNU differences that catch people out.

Quiz and exercises

Each lesson ends with a short quiz, with an explanation for every answer, and practical exercises. The learner submits an answer and ASK AI checks it against a marking guide held on the server.

ASK AI as tutor

Questions asked from a lesson go to ASK AI with that lesson as context. It teaches rather than just answers, and points to the official documentation for the product.

Progress

Completed lessons and quiz results are saved on the learner's own machine.

Official sources

Every subject links to documentation published by the product owner or maintainer: Apple, the Linux kernel man pages, GNU Bash, the Z Shell manual and others.

Linked to the platform

Lessons show where each skill is used in RunShore's own scripts, so learning connects to working code.
